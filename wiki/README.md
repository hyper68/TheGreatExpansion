## 🔧 Mod Overview

The Great Expansion is a comprehensive Minecraft mod that adds:
- **15+ New Ores** including Bauxite, Cassiterite, Galena, Ilmenite, and more
- **Multiple Tool & Armor Sets** with unique properties
- **Industrial Machines** for automation and processing
- **New Biomes** including frozen tundras and unique landscapes
- **Building Materials** for creative construction
- **Energy Systems** including power generation and storage
- **Fluid Processing** for advanced crafting
- **And Much More!**

## 🌟 Featured Content

### New Ore Processing
TGE introduces a multi-stage ore processing system that allows you to get more resources from your mining:
1. Mine raw ores
2. Crush them in machines
3. Smelt or refine for pure materials
4. Craft advanced items

### Power Generation
Generate power using various methods:
- Propane Generators for portable power
- Induction Furnaces for efficient smelting
- Advanced energy storage systems

### Custom Biomes
Explore new frozen biomes with unique flora, fauna, and resources.
