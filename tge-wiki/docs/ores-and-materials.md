## ⚙️ Metal Ores

### 🔹 Bauxite
**The aluminum ore - your gateway to TGE**

- **Y-Level:** -16 to 128
- **Frequency:** 7 veins per chunk, 9 ore per vein
- **Biomes:** All overworld biomes
- **Drops:** Bauxite (block)
- **Tool Required:** Iron pickaxe or better
- **Smelting:** Bauxite → Aluminum Ingot

**Uses:**
- Aluminum tools and equipment
- Machine components
- Lightweight building materials
- Electrical components

**Finding Tips:** Very common, mine anywhere you'd find iron or coal.

---

### 🔹 Cassiterite Ore
**Source of tin for bronze and electronics**

- **Drops:** Cassiterite Ore (block)
- **Tool Required:** Stone pickaxe or better
- **Smelting:** Cassiterite Ore → Tin Ingot

**Uses:**
- Bronze alloys (combine with copper)
- Electrical wiring
- Circuit components
- Machine parts

**Lore:** Cassiterite is the primary ore of tin, used historically for bronze-making.

---

### 🔹 Galena Ore
**Lead source for ammunition and protection**

- **Y-Level:** Underground
- **Biomes:** All overworld
- **Drops:** Galena Ore (block)
- **Tool Required:** Iron pickaxe or better
- **Smelting:** Galena Ore → Lead Ingot

**Uses:**
- Lead Arrows (special ammunition)
- Radiation shielding
- Heavy machinery components
- Weights and ballasts

**Special:** Lead items have unique properties like increased knockback.

---

### 🔹 Ilmenite Ore
**Rare titanium ore for end-game gear**

- **Y-Level:** Deep underground
- **Biomes:** All overworld
- **Rarity:** Rare
- **Drops:** Ilmenite Ore (block)
- **Tool Required:** Diamond pickaxe or better
- **Smelting:** Ilmenite Ore → Titanium Ingot

**Uses:**
- Titanium Armor (high protection)
- Titanium Sword (high damage)
- Titanium Axe (efficient)
- Advanced machine components

**Note:** One of the best armor sets in the mod!

---

### 🔹 Cinnabar Ore
**Mercury ore for advanced chemistry**

- **Y-Level:** Underground
- **Biomes:** All overworld
- **Drops:** Cinnabar Ore (block)
- **Tool Required:** Iron pickaxe or better
- **Smelting:** Cinnabar Ore → Mercury Ingot

**Uses:**
- Chemical processing
- Thermometers and gauges
- Special alloys
- Potion ingredients

**Warning:** Handle mercury items with care!

---

### 🔹 Permafrost Iron Ore
**Cold-hardened iron from frozen lands**

- **Y-Level:** Surface to underground
- **Biomes:** Snowbound, WildTundra, frozen biomes
- **Drops:** Permafrost Iron Ore (block)
- **Tool Required:** Stone pickaxe or better
- **Smelting:** Same as regular iron

**Uses:**
- Same as vanilla iron
- Cold-resistant properties
- Special machine components

**Finding Tips:** Found abundantly in frozen biomes - great early source of iron!

---

## 💎 Gem Ores

### 🔹 Aqualith Ore
**Rare aquatic gem with water affinity**

- **Y-Level:** Underwater deposits
- **Biomes:** Oceans, rivers, deep water
- **Rarity:** Rare
- **Drops:** Aqualith Ore (block)
- **Tool Required:** Iron pickaxe or better
- **Processing:** 
  - Aqualith Ore → Aqualith (gem)
  - Aqualith → Aqualith Block (for storage)

**Uses:**
- Aqualith Tools (water efficiency)
- Aqualith Armor (water breathing, swim speed)
- Decorative blocks
- Underwater construction

**Special Properties:**
- Tools mine faster underwater
- Armor provides water breathing
- Increased swim speed with full set

**Finding Tips:** 
- Explore ocean floors and underwater caves
- Look near coral reefs and underwater structures
- Use Potion of Water Breathing for extended searches

---

### 🔹 Cryonite Ore
**Frozen crystal from icy depths**

- **Y-Level:** Underground in cold biomes
- **Biomes:** Snowbound, frozen biomes
- **Drops:** Cryonite Ore (block)
- **Tool Required:** Iron pickaxe or better

**Uses:**
- Ice-based tools
- Freezing mechanisms
- Cold storage systems
- Winter-themed builds

**Special:** Has cooling properties useful for certain machines.

## 🏭 Refined Materials

### Metal Ingots

#### **Aluminum Ingot**
- **From:** Bauxite ore
- **Smelting:** 1 Bauxite → 1 Aluminum Ingot
- **Uses:** Lightweight tools, machine frames, wiring

#### **Tin Ingot**
- **From:** Cassiterite ore
- **Smelting:** 1 Cassiterite → 1 Tin Ingot
- **Uses:** Electronics, alloys, circuits

#### **Lead Ingot**
- **From:** Galena ore
- **Smelting:** 1 Galena → 1 Lead Ingot
- **Uses:** Ammunition, shielding, weights

#### **Titanium Ingot**
- **From:** Ilmenite ore
- **Smelting:** 1 Ilmenite → 1 Titanium Ingot
- **Uses:** High-end armor, weapons, machines

#### **Mercury Ingot**
- **From:** Cinnabar ore
- **Smelting:** 1 Cinnabar → 1 Mercury Ingot
- **Uses:** Thermometers, special alloys, chemistry

#### **Steel Ingot**
- **From:** Iron + Carbon processing
- **Crafting:** Requires Induction Furnace
- **Recipe:** Iron Ingot + Steel Powder → Steel Ingot
- **Uses:** Strong tools, machine frames, advanced builds

#### **Silicon Ingot**
- **From:** Silicon Dust processing
- **Smelting:** Silicon Dust → Silicon Ingot
- **Uses:** Electronics, solar panels, circuits

## ⚗️ Processing Guide

### Basic Smelting
Most ores can be smelted in a vanilla furnace:
```
Ore Block → Furnace → Ingot
```

### Advanced Processing

#### **Crushing → Smelting**
Get more yield with a Crusher:
```
1 Ore → Crusher → 2 Dust
2 Dust → Furnace → 2 Ingots
(100% yield increase!)
```

#### **Steel Production**
Multi-stage steel creation:
```
1. Mine Iron Ore
2. Smelt → Iron Ingot
3. Craft Steel Powder (Iron + Carbon)
4. Induction Furnace: Iron + Steel Powder → Steel Ingot
```

#### **Silicon Processing**
From sand to ingots:
```
1. Sand → Crusher → Silicon Dust
2. Silicon Dust → Furnace → Silicon Ingot
3. Silicon Ingot → Crafting → Silicon Sheets
```

## 💡 Tips & Strategies

### Early Game
1. **Focus on Bauxite** - Easy to find and useful
2. **Explore Frozen Biomes** - Permafrost Iron is abundant
3. **Mark Ore Locations** - Note rare ore positions on your map
4. **Build Storage** - You'll collect lots of materials

### Mid Game
1. **Set Up Crusher** - Double your ore yield
2. **Find Aqualith** - Water breathing is extremely useful
3. **Process Steel** - Required for advanced machines
4. **Stockpile Rare Ores** - Titanium and rare gems take time to find

### Late Game
1. **Automate Processing** - Set up automatic ore processing
2. **Create Ore Farms** - If possible with other mods
3. **Build Material Storage** - Organized system for all resources
4. **Master Alloys** - Create specialized materials

### Mining Efficiency
- **Branch Mine** at Y=0 for most ores
- **Explore Caves** for natural ore exposure
- **Use Fortune** enchantment on gem ores
- **Silk Touch** for ore relocation
