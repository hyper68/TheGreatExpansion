# Recipes

This page covers crafting and processing recipes in The Great Expansion.

## Ore Processing

### Direct Smelting
All ore blocks can be smelted directly:
- Input: 1 Ore Block
- Output: 1 Ingot
- Time: 10 seconds
- Fuel: 1 item

### Crusher Processing
For double yield:
1. Crush ore: 1 Ore Block → 2 Dust
2. Smelt dust: 2 Dust → 2 Ingots
Total yield: 200% (double)

---

## Steel Production

### Steel Ingot
Steel requires multi-step processing:
1. Obtain Iron Ingot (smelting iron ore)
2. Craft Steel Powder (iron + carbon)
3. Process in Induction Furnace: Iron Ingot + Steel Powder → Steel Ingot

---

## Aluminum Processing

### Aluminum Ingot
Simple smelting process:
- Input: Bauxite
- Output: Aluminum Ingot
- Time: 10 seconds

---

## Titanium Processing

### Titanium Ingot
Rare material processing:
- Input: Ilmenite Ore
- Output: Titanium Ingot
- Time: 10 seconds
- Note: Ilmenite is rare

---

## Oil Refining

### Refinery Processing
Crude oil → Refinery → Products:
- Gasoline
- Diesel
- Propane

Each product has different uses in power generation and crafting.

---

## Tool Crafting

All tools follow standard Minecraft patterns:
- Pickaxe: 3 material + 2 sticks
- Axe: 3 material + 2 sticks
- Sword: 2 material + 1 stick
- Shovel: 1 material + 2 sticks
- Hoe: 2 material + 2 sticks

---

## Armor Crafting

Standard armor patterns:
- Helmet: 5 material
- Chestplate: 8 material
- Leggings: 7 material
- Boots: 4 material
Total per set: 24 material

---

## Machine Components

### Machine Frame
Base component for machines, requires:
- Steel Beams
- Iron Ingots
- Redstone

### Steel Beams
Crafted from Steel Ingots in standard pattern.
