# Machines

The Great Expansion adds several machines for ore processing, power generation, and automation.

## Processing Machines

### Crusher
The **Crusher** is a machine that processes ore blocks and other materials.

#### Function
- Crushes ore blocks into 2 dust/powder
- Doubles ore yield when combined with smelting
- Processes various materials

#### Crafting
Requires iron ingots, piston, and redstone.

#### Usage
Place ore blocks in the input slot. The crusher will process them into dust, which can then be smelted into ingots at a 2:1 ratio compared to direct smelting.

---

### Induction Furnace
The **Induction Furnace** is an advanced smelting machine.

#### Function
- Smelts items faster than a standard furnace
- Uses energy (FE) instead of fuel
- Required for certain advanced recipes

#### Crafting
Requires steel beams, machine frame, furnace, and redstone.

#### Energy
- Energy capacity: 400,000 FE
- Max receive rate: 200 FE/t
- Max extract rate: 200 FE/t

---

### Refinery
The **Refinery** converts crude oil into usable fuels.

#### Function
- Processes crude oil
- Produces gasoline, diesel, and propane

#### Outputs
- Gasoline
- Diesel
- Propane

---

### Germination Tank
The **Germination Tank** accelerates plant growth.

#### Function
- Speeds up plant growth
- Automated farming system
- Requires water and power

---

### Pressure Cooker
The **Pressure Cooker** processes food and materials under pressure.

#### Function
- Cooks food items
- Processes certain materials
- Requires power

---

### Ice Cutter
The **Ice Cutter** processes ice blocks.

#### Function
- Cuts ice blocks into ice bricks
- Processes various ice-based materials
- Does not require power

---

### Jewelry Workbench
The **Jewelry Workbench** is used for crafting jewelry and accessories.

#### Function
- Crafts jewelry items
- Special crafting interface

---

### Filler
The **Filler** automates block placement.

#### Function
- Places blocks automatically
- Fill mode: fills empty space
- Clear mode: removes blocks
- Replace mode: swaps blocks

---

## Power Generation

### Propane Generator
The **Propane Generator** generates power from propane fuel.

#### Function
- Burns propane to generate FE
- Output: Variable FE/tick
- Fuel: Filled Propane Tank

---

### Lavaleer
The **Lavaleer** generates power from lava.

#### Function
- Converts lava into energy
- High power output
- Fuel: Lava buckets

---

## Storage

### Reinforced Chest
The **Reinforced Chest** provides secure storage.

#### Properties
- Capacity: 54 slots
- Explosion resistant
- Made from steel plates

---

### Water Tank
The **Water Tank** stores water for machines.

#### Properties
- Capacity: 16,000 mB per block
- Can be stacked vertically
- Auto-fills from rain

---

## Machine Properties

### Energy Systems
Most machines use Forge Energy (FE) as their power source:
- Standard capacity: 400,000 FE
- Standard I/O: 200 FE/tick

### Crafting Requirements
Common materials for machine crafting:
- Steel Beams
- Machine Frame
- Iron Ingots
- Redstone

---

## Data Values

Machine block IDs use the namespace `the_great_expansion:` followed by the machine name.

Examples:
- `the_great_expansion:crusher`
- `the_great_expansion:induction_furnace`
- `the_great_expansion:refinery`
