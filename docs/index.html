About

Hyper Mods is a modding studio dedicated to creating high-quality Minecraft modifications. We focus on enhancing gameplay through carefully designed features that maintain the spirit of vanilla Minecraft while introducing meaningful new content. Every mod we create is built with attention to balance, quality, and the player experience.

Products

The Great Expansion

A comprehensive modification that expands Minecraft with new ores, items, and crafting recipes. The Great Expansion introduces carefully balanced resources and mechanics designed to enhance mining and crafting while respecting vanilla gameplay.

Key Features

  • New ore generation including Bauxite and Cassiterite
  • Expanded crafting system with balanced recipes
  • Custom tools and equipment progression
  • Seamless integration with vanilla mechanics
